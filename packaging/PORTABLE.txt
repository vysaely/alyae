Alyae, portable

Unzip this folder anywhere you can write: your Documents, the Desktop, a USB stick.
Start alyae.exe. Nothing is installed and nothing is written outside this folder:
accounts and settings stay in the "data" folder beside it (the file "portable" tells Alyae
so; delete it and Alyae uses %APPDATA%\Alyae instead). The Log in window keeps its browser
profile in the temp folder and removes it when it closes.

Your accounts from another PC: copy alyae.db and vault.key from that PC's data folder
(%APPDATA%\Alyae, or its portable "data" folder) into this "data" folder while Alyae is closed.
vault.key unlocks the saved passwords and cookies: keep the two files together and private.

Needs: Windows 10 or 11 with the Roblox app installed for Join, and Microsoft Edge WebView2
(part of Windows 11 and current Windows 10) for Log in. Windows may warn that the program is
from an unknown publisher: More info, Run anyway.
